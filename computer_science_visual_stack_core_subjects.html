Operating System (OS)

The Conductor of All Hardware and Apps

In Simple Words (ELI5)

If your computer was a busy airport, the Operating System is Air Traffic Control. It makes sure games, music players, and browsers take turns using the CPU runway without crashing into each other.

Where it lives in your computer:

Runs as the privileged Kernel inside RAM, sitting directly between software applications and the physical CPU chip & hard drive.
